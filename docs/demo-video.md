# The Clock In demo video

Target **2:45**, hard cap 3:00. It has to show the real app on a real phone: the judges score
"completion" from this video, and the rules say a video nobody can install is not a submission.

Built in `video/` like the film: the film's first 20 seconds as the hook, then phone recordings
inside the film's `Phone` frame, narrated in the same voice (Gemini TTS, Sulafat, one take),
then the film's end card.

## Shot list for the phone (Wednesday)

**Before recording**
- Phone: Do Not Disturb on, battery above 50%, the clock and status bar clean, brightness up,
  dark mode **off** (the film is cream). Font size default.
- Recorder: Android's built-in screen recorder, 1080p, **no audio**, touches shown off.
- Wallet: Phantom or Solflare with ~$10 USDC and ~0.02 SOL on mainnet.
- KEPT 1.1.0 installed fresh, never opened.
- A second person for the circle scenes (shots 11–14): a friend with Android and KEPT
  installed, or a second phone. They join through your invite link before shot 11.

**Takes.** Each is a separate recording, so a fumble costs one take, not the whole run. Move
slowly, and hold still for two seconds at the start and end of each.

1. **Open & onboard.** Open KEPT → name → goal → promise "$20, every Friday" → finish on the
   empty rings.
2. **Connect.** Tap Connect → the wallet opens → approve → back in KEPT, "Wallet verified".
3. **Shelves.** Invest tab, rest on Public markets, scroll slowly to Crypto, rest, scroll to
   Private markets.
4. **Buy SOL, $1.** Solana → Buy → type 1 → let the quote settle → Review → approve in the
   wallet → wait on the screen until the ring closes and the kept moment plays. Don't cut
   the wait; it gets trimmed in the edit.
5. **Solscan.** From the confirmation (or the wallet's activity), open the transaction on
   solscan.io: the signer is your wallet, the SOL arrives in it.
6. **Circle, your side.** Circle tab: your kept post, no amount anywhere on it.
7. **Circle, friend's side.** On the friend's phone: your post arrives; they react 👏.
   On yours: the reaction arrives. Then you nudge the friend's open week.
8. **Widget.** Home screen → add the KEPT widget → it shows this week's closed ring.
9. **Portfolio & sell.** Portfolio (private to you) → SOL → Sell → show the sheet. Selling is
   optional; showing the sheet is enough.
10. **Awards.** The awards screen with "First promise".

Also buy **$1 of a stock** (SPYx) in a separate take. It isn't in the cut, but it is the
device pass for the stock path.

## Notes

- The week can be kept only once. Take 4 is the one that keeps it, so do takes 1–3 first and
  get take 4 right; a second $1 buy is fine as a retake, it simply doesn't close the ring again.
- Amounts appear on your own screens (buy sheet, portfolio). That's intended: the point the
  video makes is that the **circle** never sees them.
- No claims of returns, users or rankings anywhere in the video.
